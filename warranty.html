The NEVORA Promise

Arrives perfect,
or we make it right.

Chocolate is fragile and heat-sensitive. Almost everything that can go wrong between our workshop and your door is caused by our packaging or our courier, not by you. So fixing it is our job.

Last updated 20 September 2026 · Applies to all orders placed through nevora.com

30 days To make a claim, counted from delivery
We pay Return and reshipping costs are always ours
5 days From approved claim to resolution
Your choice Replacement, refund or store credit

What we cover

  • Chocolate that arrives melted or bloomed after heat in transit
  • Bars that arrive crushed, opened or with a broken seal
  • Manufacturing or production defects
  • The wrong item, or a bar missing from your order
  • Any bar delivered with less than 3 months of shelf life left

What we don't cover

  • Chocolate stored above 20 °C or in direct sunlight after reaching you in good condition
  • Accidental damage or misuse after delivery
  • Personal taste: finding our 70% Intense too bitter is not a defect
  • Change of mind on an opened bar: chocolate is a food product, so we cannot take it back

How to make a claim

  1. Step 01

    Write to us

    Email contact@nevora.com within 30 days of delivery, or message the WhatsApp number on your order confirmation.

  2. Step 02

    Send three things

    Your order number, a photo of the bar and its wrapper, and one line describing what went wrong.

  3. Step 03

    We reply in 2 business days

    For melted, bloomed or crushed bars the photo is enough, so we never ask you to ship chocolate back. If we need a bar returned to inspect a defect, we send a prepaid courier label.

  4. Step 04

    You choose the outcome

    Replacement, refund or store credit, whichever you prefer, confirmed and resolved within 5 business days.

Every claim is logged against the batch number on your wrapper. If one batch generates repeat claims, we pull it from stock and trace it back to the production run. The same traceability that tells you which farm grew your cacao also tells us where something went wrong.

Full policy. NEVORA guarantees that every bar reaches you in the condition we made it. If your chocolate arrives melted, bloomed, crushed, opened, incorrect, or close to its best-before date, write to contact@nevora.com within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo, and we will replace it, refund it, or issue store credit, whichever you prefer, within 5 business days. NEVORA covers all return and reshipping costs on a valid claim; you never pay to send back a bar that was wrong. This guarantee does not cover chocolate stored above 20 °C after delivery, accidental damage, or a bar that simply was not to your personal taste. Because chocolate is a food product we cannot accept opened bars back for change of mind, but if something is genuinely wrong with your NEVORA bar, we will always make it right.

Something wrong with your order?

Write to us with your order number and a photo. We answer every claim within two business days.
