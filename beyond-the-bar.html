How a corporate order runs

  1. Step 01

    Send the brief

    How many boxes, which size, when you need them, and whether it is branded. Three lines is enough to start.

  2. Step 02

    Quote in 2 days

    A written quote with the unit price, the print cost and the delivery date. Nothing moves until you approve it.

  3. Step 03

    Approve the sample

    For branded orders we build one real box and send you a photo — or the box itself, in Bogotá — before we print the run.

  4. Step 04

    We produce and deliver

    To one address, to several offices, or to each recipient's home. You get the tracking; we handle anything that goes wrong.

03 — For a room full of people

Tastings & Workshops

Put 40% and 70% cacao side by side and the difference is obvious within a minute. We build sessions around that moment: a guided tasting, a bean-to-bar workshop, or a team session at your own office.

Guided Tasting

COP $85,000 / person

60 minutes, all three bars, tasted in order. You learn what the cacao percentage actually changes, and why Origin tastes of coffee and salt.

  • 8–20 people
  • Host, tasting mat and notes card
  • Everyone takes a bar home
Full experience

Bean-to-Bar Workshop

COP $165,000 / person

2.5 hours from raw bean to a bar you temper, mould and wrap yourself. Hands in the chocolate, not a slideshow.

  • 6–14 people
  • Roasted beans, nibs and tempering at the table
  • Your own wrapped bar to take with you

Team Session

COP $110,000 / person

90 minutes at your office — a tasting built as a team activity, with the supply-chain story behind the bars for teams who care where things come from.

  • From 10 people
  • We bring everything, including the kit

What a session includes

  • A host who works with the cacao, not a hired presenter
