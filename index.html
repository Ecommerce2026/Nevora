Our Story

Two Origins, One Craft.

NEVORA was founded on a simple belief: the world's finest cacao deserves the world's finest craftsmanship. We source directly from Colombian farmers and cooperatives, then shape every bar with the precision and restraint of Swiss-inspired chocolate-making.

Where most chocolate hides its origin behind a wrapper, NEVORA puts it front and center: the region, the farmer, the process. It's not just another chocolate bar. It's a chocolate discovery experience.
